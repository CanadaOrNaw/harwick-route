# Handbook ownership

This shelf explains safe use, architecture, maintenance and evidence limits for Harwick Route. It is private operational documentation, not a source for public company claims.

## Inputs and outputs

Review the root `AGENTS.md` and `CONTEXT.md`, current implementation and [verification status](status.md). Company records/reports stay at their owner; do not copy customer, financial or private record rows here.

Plain Markdown pages and `sidebar.json` here are canonical reader inputs to the one shared documentation website. The older `_generated/` HTML/PDF/receipt files are retained generated products, not edited or used as proof of current website acceptance. The former four-page manual is expanded into task-specific pages; [index](index.md) and [navigation](guide.md) expose the routes.

## Review and publication

The private verification manifest and receipt live outside this shelf in `docs/verification/`. They account for sources, exclusions, interface coverage, safe examples and exact reviewed inputs. Authors leave their receipt changes-required; an independent reviewer checks source claims and reader tasks. Byte hashes alone establish neither correctness nor acceptance.

The shared website owner owns assembly and release. Public app deployment must exclude the handbook and verification data. [Source review](maintenance/source-review-and-status.md) explains change impact; [collaboration](collaboration.md) explains the company approval boundary.
